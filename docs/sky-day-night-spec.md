# Painterly sky and day-night cycle

## Objective
Integrate generated painterly sky artwork matching Ashveil's character/environment art into the default first-zone route, with a continuous 24-hour visual cycle lasting 600 real seconds by default and a configurable duration.

## Non-goals
Weather, seasons, gameplay/time-gated quests, authoritative networking, persisted shared time, legacy renderer changes, character modifications, production deployment.

## User refinements

- Night must stay readable, in the spirit of WoW/FFXIV: cool moonlit atmosphere without black terrain, lost path edges or silhouette-only characters. Validate midnight and twilight in game; sky darkness and ground illumination are separate controls.
- Allow upward camera look to inspect clouds/celestial objects, preserving a ground-safe physical camera position and stable movement heading. Reset and overview retain their behavior.
- Give the separately rendered sun and moon moderately large storybook scale with soft radial edges, while retaining terrain occlusion and avoiding gameplay glare or additional lights.

Upward camera contract: requested pitch clamps to [-1.35, 1.05], physical eye orbit uses max(pitch, 0.16) and the existing collision/ground-floor path. For requested pitch below 0.16 only, derive a unit view direction from the actual camera-to-character-target vector after eye interpolation; add elevation lift 0.16 - pitch, capped below zenith at 1.45 radians, preserving horizontal bearing (fall back to -sin(yaw), -cos(yaw) if horizontal bearing is degenerate). Aim at eye plus that direction. Normal pitch uses existing lookAt; overview bypasses this helper. Tests verify continuous threshold, collision-distance scale invariance, finite degenerate cases and no horizontal movement-heading flip. Returning to landing resets pitch to 0.42.

## Readability reference check

Player discussions show differing preferences, not a universal darkness target: [WoW night feedback](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-at-night/1271181) includes visibility concerns, while [FFXIV lighting feedback](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/516840) describes discomfort from both deep darkness and aggressive bright sources. These are anecdotal reports. The [official FFXIV brightness explanation](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/topics/detail/08d9caee9369f32169c8f881b219d46241037cb6) confirms that character lighting and environmental light require balancing, but concerns housing and does not establish outdoor night values. No official numeric outdoor-night target was verified for WoW. Adopted lesson: follow this user's explicit readable-night requirement, retain ambient fill and restrained moonlight, and use live Ashveil screenshots to tune the result; do not copy unverified lighting constants or claim an exact MMO match.
