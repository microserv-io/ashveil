# Main scenario quests

> **Draft status:** Original opening-chapter draft. Names, props and class-facing
> strings are provisional. The player is `{player}`; dialogue assumes no race,
> gender, class, family, birthplace or reason for knowing Bracken Hollow.

M01-M07 provide Story-dungeon readiness without mandatory side quests. Later Early
Access releases continue the same main scenario and level cap, rather than starting
new seasons. Experience earned before the player chooses a class in M03 is held as
pending character credit, then assigned once to that first class on confirmation;
changing weapons cannot duplicate it.

## Opening cinematic

**Target length:** 50-55 seconds. **Playback:** after character creation as an
authored presentation. Whole-scene replay skip only; no player choice or missable
information.

**00:00-00:07 — Bracken Hollow.** Morning: ochre roofs, green washing, mill ribbons
beside a cream-stone channel. One ribbon slows and fades to grey. Birds leave without
calling.

**00:07-00:15 — The change.** Behind `{player}` among a travelling group, colour
drains from climbing flowers. Nothing burns and ash does not fall. A wooden wheel
stops although water still moves beneath it.

**Mara:** “East road. Take only who you can reach. Move.”

**00:15-00:27 — Flight.** `{player}` shoulders a handcart; Iven binds a stranger's
arm; Tavi looks back; Len lifts a fallen marker so others can pass. The grey quiet
advances through grass as the group runs toward the River Alder.

**Iven, breathless:** “It isn't smoke.”

**Mara:** “Then save the questions for the other bank.”

**00:27-00:41 — The crossing.** Refugees crowd a ferry and rope footbridge. A
panicked pack beast strikes the rail and one anchor tears loose. `{player}`, Mara,
Iven and Tavi reach safety as the bridge swings downstream. Len and several adults
remain on the Bracken side.

**Len, calling across:** “Go! We know the lower road!”

**00:41-00:52 — Alderbank.** `{player}` rises from wet shingle among scattered packs.
Lanterns glow beneath a small refuge's eaves. Edda rings a hand bell. Mara turns
from the broken crossing.

**Mara, quiet but firm:** “Count the living. Then we go back for the rest.”

Title card: **ASHVEIL**. Control begins with no weapon equipped. M01 and M02 appear
together; no hostile can enter this riverbank area.

## M01 — Who Made It Across?

- **Giver:** Mara, group organiser
- **Location:** Alderbank river landing
- **Prerequisite:** Opening cinematic
- **Availability / bundle:** Available with M02 in one non-combat space; complete
  both in either order to open M03.
- **Journal summary:** The crossing scattered people along the safe bank. Help Mara
  find everyone who made it across before the river carries away signs of them.

**Offer dialogue**

**Mara:** “Three people were swept below the landing. Check the willow shelf, ferry
post and driftwood. Call before you touch anyone. Frightened people wake fighting.”

**Player reply:** “I’ll search the bank and bring back whoever I find.”

**Mara:** “Good. Point them toward Edda's bell. If they cannot walk, mark the place
with one of these blue ties and Iven will come.”

**Objectives**

1. Take Mara's bundle of blue rescue ties.
2. Clear two branches and assist the survivor beneath the willow shelf.
3. Throw the rescue line from marked footing to the survivor at the ferry post.
4. Speak to the dazed survivor in the driftwood; place a blue tie for Iven's helpers.
5. Return to Mara at the landing.

**Objective and event dialogue**

- Willow survivor: “I can stand. Just—give me a moment where the ground stays put.”
- Ferry-post survivor, once ashore: “Len kept the rope up until the last boat. Did
  you see Len cross?”
- **Player:** “Not yet. Go to the bell. We’re still counting.”
- Dazed survivor: “Safe bank?”
- **Player:** “Safe bank. Help is coming.”
- After the third rescue, Mara calls from the landing: “That is everyone below the
  bend. Bring me the count.”

**In-progress reminder**

**Mara:** “Three missing below the landing: willow shelf, ferry post, driftwood.
Blue ties for anyone who cannot walk.”

**Complete / turn-in dialogue**

**Player:** “All three are alive. One needs carrying. No sign of Len.”

**Mara:** “Alive is a good count. We keep it. Len chose the lower road and knows how
to hold a frightened group together. That buys us time, not certainty.”

**Reward intent:** Pending main-scenario experience and basic social access recorded
directly by quest completion, with narrative recognition among refuge organisers.

**Replay / early-credit / group notes:** Rescue states are one-time. Nearby party
members share credit without duplicating survivors. Persistent rescues made after
M01 became available grant early credit. There is no combat.

## M02 — A Place to Breathe

- **Giver:** Iven, healer
- **Location:** Alderbank river landing and refuge awning
- **Prerequisite:** Opening cinematic
- **Availability / bundle:** Available with M01 on the same non-combat route; both
  are required for M03.
- **Journal summary:** Iven's medical satchel washed downriver during the crossing.
  Recover it, then help settle the wounded under Alderbank's awning.

**Offer dialogue**

**Iven:** “I have six hands' worth of work and, inconveniently, two hands. My brown
satchel went into the river. It has red stitching and dislikes water almost as much
as I do.”

**Player reply:** “Where should I look?”

**Iven:** “Reeds below the ferry post. Bring it closed. Then I’ll show you what can
be trusted after a soaking.”

**Objectives**

1. Search up to three reed clumps below the ferry post; find Iven's satchel by the
   third.
2. Bring the satchel to Iven under the refuge awning.
3. Following Iven's prompts, distribute dry wraps to three wounded adults.
4. Refill the painted water basin from Alderbank's hand pump.
5. Speak with Iven.

**Objective and event dialogue**

- On finding the satchel, **Iven, at distance:** “Hold it above the mud, please. The
  mud already has everything it needs.”
- First patient: “Save the wrap for someone worse.”
- **Iven:** “A generous infection is still an infection. Take the wrap.”
- At the water basin, **Iven:** “Slowly. Breathing first, drinking second. Panic
  makes poor medicine.”
- When the last patient is settled, **Iven:** “There. No miracles. Just fewer things
  getting worse.”

**In-progress reminder**

**Iven:** “Red stitching, downriver reeds. If you have the satchel, bring it here;
if you have the wraps, three people still need them.”

**Complete / turn-in dialogue**

**Player:** “The basin is full and everyone has a dry wrap.”

**Iven:** “Then they have a place to breathe. That sounds small until breathing is
the whole day. Thank you, `{player}`. Mara wants you by the store shed when your
hands are free.”

**Reward intent:** Pending main-scenario experience and a basic field dressing or
equivalent recovery tutorial item, subject to the recovery-system decision.

**Replay / early-credit / group notes:** Reed searches advance a deterministic count.
Care uses personal state on one ambient patient population. A satchel found after
M02 becomes available grants early credit. No class skill or combat is required.

## M03 — Something to Carry

- **Giver:** Mara
- **Location:** Alderbank store shed and fenced practice yard
- **Prerequisite:** M01 and M02 complete
- **Availability / bundle:** Standalone; completion opens M04 and M05 together.
- **Journal summary:** The road beyond Alderbank is unsafe. Examine the salvaged
  weapons, choose a first class, and learn its basic action in the practice yard.

**Offer dialogue**

**Mara:** “You crossed with empty hands. That was sensible then. It won’t be on the
road. These weapons were left at the refuge by travellers and ward crews. Each asks
something different of the person carrying it.”

**Player reply:** “I want to understand the choice before I make it.”

**Mara:** “The rack explains each class, role and first action. Try what interests
you and change your mind as often as you need. A first choice is a beginning, not a
sentence.”

**Objectives**

1. Inspect three configured launch weapons; each card shows class fantasy, group
   role, range and basic-action preview without narrative defining the roster.
2. Equip one salvaged class weapon. This establishes the first active class.
3. Use that weapon's basic action three times on marked practice bundles. Any valid
   action and any target order count.
4. Optionally return to the rack to test or select another weapon before confirming.
5. Confirm the chosen weapon with Mara.

**Objective and event dialogue**

- First inspection, **Mara:** “Weight tells you less than purpose. Read the card,
  then picture the work.”
- First practice action, **Mara:** “Again. Learn where the motion ends; that is when
  the next decision begins.”
- Third practice action, **Mara:** “Enough for the road. Mastery can wait until after
  breakfast.”
- Optional selection prompt: **“Carry this weapon and begin as {class display
  name}? You can change your choice at this rack before turn-in and can learn other
  classes later.”**
- **Player confirmation:** “This is the one I’ll carry.”

**In-progress reminder**

**Mara:** “Inspect the rack, choose what fits, then use its basic action three times
on the practice bundles. Nothing here punishes a second thought.”

**Complete / turn-in dialogue**

**Mara:** “Keep your grip easy and your eyes up. A weapon gives you a way to act; it
doesn’t decide who you are. Hessa and I have two pieces of work outside the yard.
Take them together.”

**Reward intent:** Chosen weapon, first-class unlock, basic kit, M03 experience and
one-time assignment of all pending pre-class quest experience, including completed
side quests, to that class. The rack remains available; later class acquisition
rules remain open.

**Replay / early-credit / group notes:** Practice is personal and safe; parties do
not compete for targets. Inspections and actions after availability grant early
credit, but class choice always requires confirmation. All text uses configured
class data.

## M05 — What We Could Save

- **Giver:** Hessa, wagon craftsperson
- **Location:** Alderbank east verge and stranded wagon
- **Prerequisite:** M03 complete
- **Availability / bundle:** Offered with M04 on the same wagon loop; both open
  M06/M07.
- **Journal summary:** Hessa can turn the wagon's surviving stores into equipment for
  the class you chose. Recover useful pieces before river damp and ash spoil them.

**Offer dialogue**

**Hessa:** “The wagon landed badly, but timber complains louder than it dies. I need
five sound pieces total: waxed cloth, two clasp bundles, a lining roll and the sealed
tool case. Bring what you find. I’ll fit it to the weapon you chose.”

**Player reply:** “You can make gear out of that?”

**Hessa:** “I can make gear out of an apology if it has good stitching. Go before
the damp wins.”

**Objectives**

1. Recover four loose supply pieces around the stranded wagon: waxed cloth, two clasp
   bundles and a lining roll. At least two lie on the animal-release route, and none
   require an additional combat clear beyond M04.
2. Open the wagon's jammed side locker after the nearby threat is defeated and
   retrieve the salvage tool case.
3. Deliver the recovered material bundle to Hessa at the wagon worktable.
4. Select the class-compatible starter gear presentation generated for the equipped
   weapon and watch Hessa complete the fit.
5. Equip at least one new gear piece, then speak with Hessa.

**Objective and event dialogue**

- On the first supply piece, **Hessa:** “Dry edge, strong weave. Keep that.”
- At the jammed locker, **Hessa:** “Lift, then pull. It swelled in the river.”
- Crafting beat: Hessa measures by eye, trims a ruined edge and reuses its sound
  fastening. “Salvage is deciding what still has work left in it.”
- On equip, **Hessa:** “Move your shoulders. Bend. If it pinches now, it will bite
  after a mile.”
- **Player:** “It holds.”
- **Hessa:** “High praise for honest work.”

**In-progress reminder**

**Hessa:** “Four loose pieces and the sealed tool case—five total. Search Mara’s
loop; I have no interest in making you clear it twice.”

**Complete / turn-in dialogue**

**Hessa:** “Plain, fitted and yours. Better gear will come. This will keep the road
from taking its price all at once.”

**Player:** “I’ll bring it back in one piece.”

**Hessa:** “Bring yourself back. I know how to mend the rest.”

**Reward intent:** A coherent first useful gear set matched through class data to the

**Replay / early-credit / group notes:** Supplies use unfarmable personal state. The

5. Speak with Edda at the field rally.

**Objective and event dialogue**

## Story dungeon — The Broken Waystation

**Format:** Provisional four-player Story dungeon with matchmaking, no timer and a
checkpoint after the engine. Level sync applies. Mechanics use movement, combat or
universal interact; the party never splits.

### Entrance and descent

Cream-stone steps descend beside cyan ward lines that turn violet. Tavi guides the
party to the first gate, then remains at a speaking tube; Edda uses the ward system.

**Tavi:** “Len’s boot, broad heel. Two lighter pairs. They passed this gate.”

**Edda:** “The ward will pulse before it hurts. Light gathers inward, sound drops,
then the violet edge crosses the floor. Grounding circles stay gold.”

**Party barks:**

- First player approaching the violet line: “Colour is draining from the moss.”
- Another player: “It’s still alive behind the line. Keep moving.”
- Tavi at the sealed gate: “Claw marks on this side. The beasts were trapped below
  after Len passed.”

### Encounter one — Weirback Alpha

A displaced river predator and small pack guard the gate. **Raking Rush** marks a
charge lane; **Banking Cry** gathers the pack before outward attacks; **Shingle
Toss** marks two impact circles. At low health, familiar moves repeat faster.

**Tavi, pull:** “Alpha first or pack first—either works. Just don’t let the charge
choose your ground.”

**Edda, first Raking Rush:** “Marked lane. Step clear.”

On defeat, the animals retreat through a drainage grille. A gate lever lies beneath
their nesting debris.

**Tavi:** “Driven underground, same as us. Lift the lever. Let it go.”

Universal interaction opens the service descent and activates the first shortcut
back to the entrance.

### Encounter two — The failing ward engine

Three regulators surround the engine. Vermin waves alternate with **Gathering
Current**: gold circles avoid its violet sweep. Each sweep unlocks one regulator for
any player to turn while enemies remain. Multiple players shorten the channel; a
missed interaction repeats the phase without splitting the party.

**Edda, start:** “Three regulators. Set each arrow toward the river. The current is
wrong, not dead.”

**Len, faintly through the lower pipe:** “If that is Edda shouting, tell her the
third wheel sticks.”

**Edda:** “It does not stick. Len turns it the wrong way.”

**Len:** “I’m delighted you came.”

After the third regulator, the engine's violet surge settles to controlled cyan and
the floor regains muted colour. The checkpoint activates.

**Edda:** “Stable. Not repaired—stable. Take the lower stair.”

**S20 optional hook:** A bell-stair lever beside the stable engine is interactable
whether or not S20 is accepted and opens its side chamber. Inspecting the chamber
records personal discovery flags that persist through later acceptance, so a player
who already explored it does not repeat the survey. The records add context, name no
culprit and give no combat edge. The chamber rejoins the route without a split;
unfinished steps remain available on later retellings. Tavi receives turn-in at
Alderbank.

### Lower route and survivor barks

Three knocks answer when the party uses a wall pipe. Len speaks from a barred refuge
alcove beyond the cistern.

**Len:** “Three alive. One bad ankle. Something large is between us and you.”

**Player:** “Stay behind the ward line. We’re coming through.”

**Len:** “That was already the plan.”

**S19 optional hook:** Len's named rust-red scarf with a hand-mended blue edge is
caught at the dry pipe and remains interactable whether or not S19 is accepted.
Interacting records personal discovery and recovery on the shared prop. Those flags
persist through later acceptance, allowing immediate turn-in instead of a forced
replay; an unfound scarf remains available on later retellings. It is not needed to
find Len. Len receives turn-in at Alderbank.

### Encounter three — Ash-dimmed Mossback

A cistern grazer, pale on one side after repeated ward pulses, is ash-affected but
never immune. All ordinary attacks work.

**Stillwater Sweep** is a horn-telegraphed frontal arc. **Draining Pools** creates
expanding grey slows. **Borrowed Pulse** exposes two damageable ward growths; breaking
either reduces a survivable room-wide hit. At half health, gold safe bands shift once
between repeats of the same mechanics.

**Edda, pull:** “The ward has been pouring through it. It will hurt, and it can be
hurt. Watch the horn.”

**Len, first Borrowed Pulse:** “Bright knots on its shoulder. Those weren’t there
when it chased us in.”

At defeat, the creature collapses breathing. Cyan ward light recedes from its hide;
it rises unsteadily and escapes through the opened upper sluice.

**Player bark:** “Colour’s returning.”

**Edda:** “Some. Don’t call it cured. Open the alcove.”

### Rescue, clue and exit

The party raises the refuge bar together through a universal interact. Len supports
an adult with an injured ankle; the third survivor carries their water flask. No
survivor is framed as helpless, and all three begin moving under their own power.

**Len:** “We came below when the road ward turned inward. The door sealed after us.
That creature took the worst of every pulse.”

**Second survivor:** “We heard someone in the service room before the light changed.
Metal on metal. We never saw a face.”

The work ledge holds a damp maintenance slip and the stamping ring used on it. Its
date matches the legitimate repair, but its split impression and the ring's matching
break differ from that entry's complete seal. This supports deliberate alteration,
but is not proof the ash was manufactured and identifies no culprit.

**Edda:** “Bag them together. The paper says the repair was signed. That broken ring
made this split impression, not the complete one in our record.”

**Tavi:** “Can we follow it?”

**Edda:** “We can compare it. That is what evidence earns us.”

The reopened service lift carries the entire party and all three survivors to the
waystation rally in one trip. No player remains behind. Repeat runs retell this same
scene for first-timers in mixed parties; personal completion flags suppress duplicate
quest objects and rescue rewards.
