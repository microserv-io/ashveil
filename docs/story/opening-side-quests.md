# Side quests

S01-S10 and S17-S20 are standalone; S11-S13 and S14-S16 are two
three-quest local chains. Completion persists per character through class changes.
Alternate characters undertake them as different lives. Reward quantities remain
balance work.

## S01 — The Tin Thrush

- **Giver:** Mara
- **Location:** Alderbank refuge, lower landing
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available with M01/M02 immediately after the opening
  cinematic. Safe-area quest with no combat.
- **Journal:** A small tin bird fell from Mara's bundle while everyone crossed the
  landing. Search the bank before more feet churn the mud.

**Offer**

Mara: “I count people first, baggage second. That is the right order, but a little
tin thrush slipped from my coat while we crossed. Len made it from a tea tin on a
rainy afternoon. If it reached the current, let it go. If it is still on this bank,
I would like it back.”

**Player:** “I’ll search the landing. You keep counting people.”

**Objectives**

1. Inspect the scuffed plank where Mara set down her bundles.
2. Follow three small flashes in the reeds beside the safe bank.
3. Lift the tin thrush from beneath a bent reed without snapping its wire wing.
4. Return the thrush to Mara.

**Interaction dialogue**

- At the plank: “A narrow mark runs toward the water, too straight for a boot heel.”
- At the second flash: Tavi says, “Not there. That is mica. The next glint is duller.”
- On recovery: “The tin is dented, but the tiny wing still turns.”

**Progress reminder**

Mara: “Check where the reeds lean downstream. Please don’t wade for it; one missing
person is already too many.”

**Completion**

Mara: “There you are. One wing crooked and still determined to fly. Len would say
that makes it accurate.”

**Player:** “Keep it close. We’ll bring its maker back to see it.”

- **Reward intent:** Early experience and a useful class-neutral leveling accessory,
  with no exclusive appearance or story clue.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** The persistent thrush grants early credit if found
  before acceptance. One recovery advances all nearby accepted party members without
  duplicating the item. Personal turn-in to Mara at Alderbank.

## S02 — Dry Cloth, Dry Tempers

- **Giver:** Iven
- **Location:** Alderbank refuge, covered bank shelter
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available during M01/M02; safe-area quest with no
  combat.
- **Journal:** Damp bedding and bandages were packed together during the escape.
  Help Iven sort what can be dried now and what must be kept clean.

**Offer**

Iven: “Someone packed wet blankets on top of clean cloth. A triumph of urgency over
geometry. I need the bedding hung and the sealed rolls brought here. No healer’s
training required. If a bundle is tied with blue cord, do not improve it.”

**Player:** “Blue cord stays closed. I’ll sort the rest.”

**Objectives**

1. Collect three damp bedding rolls from the baggage row.
2. Hang them on the marked lines beneath the alder canopy.
3. Carry two blue-cord cloth rolls to Iven’s treatment table.
4. Tell Iven the dry stores are separated.

**Interaction dialogue**

- At an overfilled line: Hessa says, “Leave a hand’s width. Cloth dries; heaps sulk.”
- With a sealed roll: Iven says, “Good. Still tied. My faith in labels survives.”
- After the last blanket: A child nearby says, “That red one is ours. It scratches,
  so we know it.”

**Progress reminder**

Iven: “Bedding to the lines, blue cords to me. If the knots remain shut, I may even
be pleasant.”

**Completion**

Iven: “Clean, dry, and no one decided to diagnose a blanket. Excellent work. People
will sleep warmer, which is half of healing on a night like this.”

**Player:** “Then I’ll count the scratching blanket as medicine too.”

- **Reward intent:** Early experience and a small bundle of general leveling cloth
  materials usable later by multiple production paths; no profession unlock.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Previously hung marked bedding counts on acceptance.
  Nearby accepted party members share station credit; sealed rolls remain personal.
  Turn in to Iven; completion persists through class changes.

## S03 — A Pot for Everyone

- **Giver:** Edda
- **Location:** Alderbank refuge, common cookfire
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available during M01/M02; safe-area quest with no
  combat and no crafting requirement.
- **Journal:** Edda has enough food for a plain meal, but too few hands. Fetch water,
  portion roots, and ask who needs a bowl carried to them.

**Offer**

Edda: “I can feed frightened people or answer the same six questions about blankets.
I cannot do both while stirring. Bring water from the covered barrel, pass these
roots through the cutter, then ask Iven who cannot walk over. That is kitchen work,
not a profession. Confidence will only make the knife notice you.”

**Player:** “Water, roots, and a headcount. I’ll keep my confidence modest.”

**Objectives**

1. Fill the cooking jug from Alderbank’s covered water barrel.
2. Use the hand cutter to portion four washed roots.
3. Ask Iven for the names of two people who need meals delivered.
4. Carry both covered bowls to their recipients.
5. Return the empty tray to Edda.

**Interaction dialogue**

- At the cutter: Edda says, “Flat side down. I prefer supper without fingertips.”
- First recipient: “Put it here, please. The smell reached me before my appetite did.”
- Second recipient: “Half a bowl is enough. Give the rest to whoever says it isn’t.”

**Progress reminder**

Edda: “The pot is patient. The people waiting for it are trying to be.”

**Completion**

Edda: “Every bowl returned empty. That is the closest a cook gets to applause. You
made this bank feel inhabited instead of occupied.”

**Player:** “Save me a bowl if there’s one left.”

- **Reward intent:** Early experience and a modest stack of useful travel food with
  a short leveling benefit; recipe knowledge is not required or granted.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Water and roots can receive early credit. Nearby
  accepted party members share station credit but deliver their own bowls. Turn in
  to Edda.

## S04 — Room for Quiet

- **Giver:** Iven
- **Location:** Alderbank refuge, old ferry bench
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available during M01/M02 after speaking to Iven once;
  safe-area quest with no combat.
- **Journal:** A traveler named Seli has refused food and conversation since reaching
  Alderbank. Iven asks for company without interrogation.

**Offer**

Iven: “Seli is sitting by the old ferry post. They are not injured, and they do not
owe us a tidy account of today. Take this warm cup over. Sit if invited. Silence is
allowed to do some of the work.”

**Player:** “I can bring the cup and let Seli choose the rest.”

**Objectives**

1. Take the warm cup from Iven.
2. Offer it to Seli at the ferry bench.
3. Choose to sit nearby or give Seli space.
4. Wait until Seli is ready to speak.
5. Report to Iven that Seli accepted the cup.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Seli: “You can sit. Just don’t ask what I left.”
- **Sit nearby — Player:** “I won’t ask. I can watch the river with you.”
- Seli: “All right. Tell me when the cup stops steaming.”
- **Give space — Player:** “I’ll be by the post if you want someone near.”
- Seli: “Near is good. Thank you for knowing the difference.”
- After the pause, Seli: “I can eat later. Tell the healer I said later, not never.”

**Progress reminder**

Iven: “The cup goes to Seli. Your task is presence, not extraction.”

**Completion**

Iven: “Later is a useful word. It leaves a door open without pushing anyone through.”

**Player:** “Seli chose the distance. I only kept it company.”

- **Reward intent:** Early experience and a useful restorative for leveling.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** No early credit. Both choices give identical rewards
  and outcomes. The conversation is personal and cannot be skipped by party credit.
  Turn in to Iven.

## S05 — Names on Waiting Wood

- **Giver:** Mara
- **Location:** Alderbank refuge, notice rail beneath the alders
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available during M01/M02 after the initial survivor
  count; safe-area quest with no combat.
- **Journal:** Mara is making a careful public list of people whose whereabouts are
  unknown. Confirm spellings and hang the list where additions can be made.

**Offer**

Mara: “People keep asking whether I have a list of the missing. I do, but a list can
turn uncertainty into a verdict if it is written carelessly. Ask three groups how
their people spell their names. Write ‘awaiting word’ at the top. Then hang it low
enough that anyone can add to it.”

**Player:** “I’ll record what they give me and nothing more.”

**Objectives**

1. Confirm two names with the family beside the supply awning.
2. Confirm one name with the ferryman at the upper rope.
3. Ask Seli whether they want a name included; accept either answer.
4. Title the board “Awaiting Word.”
5. Hang the board on the low hooks and speak with Mara.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Family member: “Yara, with one r. She always corrects people herself.”
- Ferryman: “Deme. Put ‘last seen upriver,’ not ‘lost.’ I saw him choosing the ridge.”
- Seli, if included: “Write Parn. Large letters, please.” Player: “I’ll write it as
  you asked.”
- Seli, if declined: “Not yet. I’ll tell you if that changes.” Player: “Then I’ll
  leave the space open.”

**Progress reminder**

Mara: “Spellings, consent, and ‘awaiting word.’ Hope deserves accurate records.”

**Completion**

Mara: “Good. It leaves room beneath every name. For a sighting, a message, a line
through the worry when someone walks in.”

**Player:** “Then we’ll keep the charcoal beside it.”

- **Reward intent:** Early experience and useful general leveling materials from
  Alderbank’s communal stores.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Earlier confirmations count if the character heard
  them. Seli’s choice does not alter rewards. Accepted party members share board
  steps; consent dialogue is personal. Turn in to Mara.

## S06 — Hessa’s Wandering Tools

- **Giver:** Hessa
- **Location:** Supply wagon at Alderbank, then the lower road
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available after M03; offered with the M04/M05 road
  outing before departure.
- **Journal:** Hessa’s repair tray opened during the flight from Bracken Hollow.
  Recover the marked tools visible along the lower road.

**Offer**

Hessa: “My wagon kept its wheels and shed everything smaller. Three tools have red
cord on the handles: a brace key, a square, and a narrow hammer. They fell this side
of the ash line. Don’t rummage in grey grass for sentiment. I can replace tools; I
cannot replace you.”

**Player:** “I’ll stay on the living side and bring back what I see.”

**Objectives**

1. Recover the brace key beside the first road rut.
2. Free the carpenter’s square from beneath a tipped handcart.
3. Retrieve the narrow hammer from the dry culvert ledge.
4. Return all three red-cord tools to Hessa at the wagon.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Brace key: “The red cord is muddy but unmistakable.”
- Handcart: Hessa calls from the road, “Lift with the spoke, not your back. The cart
  has already won one argument today.”
- Hammer: Tavi says, “That ledge is sound. The pale patch beyond it is not.”

**Progress reminder**

Hessa: “Key in the rut, square under the cart, hammer by the culvert. Red cords. No
heroics for hardware.”

**Completion**

Hessa: “All three. Now I can make the wagon stop sounding like a cupboard thrown
down stairs.”

**Player:** “I was starting to think the noise was part of its character.”

- **Reward intent:** Experience for the active class and useful class-appropriate
  equipment maintenance materials; no crafting profession or core system unlock.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Persistent tools found earlier grant credit on
  acceptance. Each recovery advances nearby accepted party members without removing
  their access. Turn in to Hessa at her current M04/M05 wagon stop.

## S07 — Pella Has Other Plans

- **Giver:** Hessa
- **Location:** Supply wagon and fern meadow off the lower road
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available after M03; offered with M04/M05 before the
  road outing leaves Alderbank.
- **Journal:** Pella the pack goat slipped her tether and is browsing toward a muddy
  hollow. Tempt her back without frightening her.

**Offer**

Hessa: “Pella can pull half a wagon and detect an unsecured knot at thirty paces.
She is in the fern meadow pretending not to know me. Take this saltleaf. Approach
from the road side so she comes toward safety, and please preserve what remains of
her dignity.”

**Player:** “I’ll tell everyone she returned by professional judgment.”

**Objectives**

1. Find Pella’s hoofprints at the meadow entrance.
2. Approach from the road side without entering the muddy hollow.
3. Offer the saltleaf when Pella looks up.
4. Walk beside Pella to the wagon tether.
5. Secure the quick-release knot and speak to Hessa.

**Interaction dialogue**

- On approach: Tavi says, “Slowly. Her ears noticed you before the rest of her did.”
- At the saltleaf: Hessa says, “There. Pure loyalty, purchased by lunch.”
- At the tether: Pella nudges the player’s shoulder, then plants all four feet.

**Progress reminder**

Hessa: “Come from the road, leaf held low. Chasing her only makes this a game, and
Pella is better at games.”

**Completion**

Hessa: “Knot holds, goat approves, wagon moves. I’ll omit the part where she outwitted
both of us.”

**Player:** “Her reputation is safe with me.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and useful travel provisions.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Earlier hoofprint discovery counts. Nearby accepted
  party members share coaxing and escort credit; a personal interaction copy avoids
  competition with other groups. Turn in to Hessa at the wagon.

## S08 — The Fence Remembers

- **Giver:** Orren
- **Location:** Alderbank road gate and first field boundary
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available after M03; offered with M04/M05 before
  departure.
- **Journal:** A field fence was opened to let Bracken Hollow’s travelers through.
  Orren wants it made safe for livestock without erasing the improvised passage.

**Offer**

Orren: “We cut the south rails yesterday so the wagon could pass. Sensible then,
troublesome now. Fit these two spare bars and tie a strip of white cloth on the post.
The cloth tells anyone behind us where the gate was. A repair can remember why it
was broken.”

**Player:** “I’ll close the field and leave the passage marked.”

**Objectives**

1. Carry two light fence bars from Orren’s stack.
2. Set the lower bar into the surviving sockets.
3. Brace the upper bar with the provided wedge.
4. Tie the white route cloth to the gatepost.
5. Test the latch and report to Orren.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Lower bar: Orren says, “Good fit. That post was old when I was short enough to
  climb it.”
- Route cloth: A traveler says, “We came through there in the dark. I didn’t know.”
- Latch test: “The gate gives once, then holds.”

**Progress reminder**

Orren: “Two bars, one wedge, white cloth last. Close it for the animals; mark it for
the people.”

**Completion**

Orren: “Strong enough for my stubborn ewe, visible enough for a tired walker. That
will do better than pretty.”

**Player:** “It can be both a fence and a sign now.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and useful general wood-and-fiber
  leveling materials, without teaching or requiring a craft.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** An earlier fence inspection counts, but repairs
  require acceptance. Nearby accepted party members share construction credit. Turn
  in to Orren at the road gate before or after the outing.

## S09 — Marks That Move

- **Giver:** Tavi
- **Location:** Alderbank lower road to the wagon halt
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available after M03; offered with M04/M05 before
  departure.
- **Journal:** Tavi wants temporary route markers placed where the safe road bends.
  Set them by landmarks that will still be visible in poor light.

**Offer**

Tavi: “The road is clear while you can see its colour. At dusk, every bend looks
reasonable. I made three hanging markers. Put them on the split alder, the flat
stone, and the old rain frame. High enough for people, low enough that we can remove
them when the route changes.”

**Player:** “I’ll place them where tired eyes will find them.”

**Objectives**

1. Hang a marker on the split alder facing Alderbank.
2. Attach a marker to the road side of the flat stone.
3. Tie a marker beneath the old rain frame.
4. Walk back far enough to verify all three sightlines.
5. Give Tavi the route check.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Split alder: Tavi says, “Turn it a finger-width left. Good. Now it catches the path.”
- Flat stone: “From this side the marker is visible without leaving the road.”
- Sightline check: A wagon traveler says, “Three pale squares in a row. I could
  follow that half asleep.”

**Progress reminder**

Tavi: “Tree, stone, frame. Then look back. A marker that works only beside itself is
decoration.”

**Completion**

Tavi: “All three read cleanly. I’ll check them each trip and take them down if the
safe ground shifts.”

**Player:** “A route should change before it becomes a habit.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and useful route cloth and general
  leveling materials.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Earlier landmark discoveries count, but placement
  requires acceptance. Nearby accepted party members share placement and sightline
  credit. Turn in to Tavi at the wagon halt or Alderbank.

## S10 — Apples for the Upper Room

- **Giver:** Nella
- **Location:** Alderbank road orchard edge and refuge loft
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Available after M03; offered with M04/M05 before
  departure.
- **Journal:** Nella’s cousins sheltering in Alderbank’s loft asked whether any of
  their early apples survived. Gather only sound windfalls and deliver them with a
  message from home.

**Offer**

Nella: “My cousins took the loft because Ren’s knee hates stairs less than damp
ground. They asked about the orchard before they asked about themselves. Bring six
sound windfalls from the grass, no shaking branches. Tell them the west row is still
green and the old ladder is still crooked.”

**Player:** “Six from the ground, and the ladder remains guilty.”

**Objectives**

1. Inspect windfalls beneath the first three trees.
2. Gather six unbruised apples from living grass.
3. Leave split fruit in Nella’s compost basket.
4. Deliver the apples and Nella’s message to Ren in the Alderbank loft.
5. Return Ren’s reply to Nella.

**Interaction dialogue**

- At a split apple: Nella says, “Basket, please. Bees have standards too.”
- Ren: “Green west row, crooked ladder. That sounds like home behaving normally.”
- Ren’s reply: “Tell Nella we’ll prune the high branches together when I’m back.”

**Progress reminder**

Nella: “Sound fruit for the loft, split fruit for the basket. The trees have given
enough without being shaken.”

**Completion**

Nella: “Pruning together. Good. Ren plans in seasons when the rest of us think in
hours. I needed that.”

**Player:** “The apples helped, but the crooked ladder did most of the work.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and useful food or gathering materials.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Earlier sound apples persist and count on acceptance.
  Nearby accepted party members share inspections; baskets are personal. Turn in to
  Nella at the orchard edge.

## S11 — Where the Water Went

- **Giver:** Orren
- **Location:** First field and irrigation headgate
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** First quest in Orren’s chain. Available after M03 and
  offered at Alderbank before the optional farmstead outing departs.
- **Journal:** The first field is drying unevenly. Walk Orren’s irrigation line and
  mark where the flow disappears.

**Offer**

Orren: “This field has been taking turns with the same stream longer than I’ve been
alive. Today the near furrows are wet and the far ones dust. Walk the channel with
these reed pins. Mark leaks, blockages, or anything that sounds hollow. Don’t fix
what you haven’t understood.”

**Player:** “I’ll map the trouble before moving it.”

**Objectives**

1. Test the flow at the stone headgate.
2. Place a reed pin beside the silted bend.
3. Place a reed pin beside the burrowed bank.
4. Tap the covered channel and mark the hollow section.
5. Meet Orren at the far-field footbridge.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Headgate: “Water reaches the first furrow with normal force.”
- Silted bend: Orren says, “That drift came from last week’s rain, not the ash. Nice
  to have an ordinary problem.”
- Hollow section: “The covering stone answers with a thin echo.”

**Progress reminder**

Orren: “Pin what you find and leave it be. Water punishes enthusiasm more quickly
than most things.”

**Completion**

Orren: “Three faults, none where I guessed. My grandmother laid this channel and
claimed it trained honesty into anyone who maintained it. Apparently it is still
working.”

**Player:** “Now we know where to put the honest work.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and useful leveling materials.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Earlier landmark inspections count. Nearby accepted
  party members share tests and pins. Turn in to Orren at the footbridge; S11 unlocks
  S12 immediately there.

## S12 — Three Small Repairs

- **Giver:** Orren
- **Location:** First-field irrigation line
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Requires S11; second quest in Orren’s chain, offered
  at the far-field footbridge without returning to Alderbank.
- **Journal:** Repair the three faults marked along Orren’s channel using the fitted
  materials in his field cache.

**Offer**

Orren: “Now we fix only what the water showed us. There are scoops in the field box,
clay wrap for the burrow, and a short lintel for the hollow cover. My grandmother’s
method was to describe every mistake as ‘a useful lesson for somebody else.’ Let us
give her fewer lessons.”

**Player:** “Silt, bank, cover—in the order the water meets them.”

**Objectives**

1. Take the fitted repair bundle from Orren’s field cache.
2. Scoop the marked silt into the settling basket.
3. Press clay wrap into the burrowed bank and smooth both edges.
4. Seat the short lintel beneath the hollow cover stone.
5. Open the headgate halfway and inspect all three repairs.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Silt basket: “Clear water threads around the bend again.”
- Clay wrap: Orren says, “Feather the edge. A hard ridge invites the next leak.”
- Lintel: “The cover settles with a low, solid note.”
- Half-flow test: Orren says, “No clouding, no sag. Leave it at half until the clay
  remembers its shape.”

**Progress reminder**

Orren: “Use the fitted pieces. If something does not sit cleanly, stop and call me.”

**Completion**

Orren: “The stream reaches the footbridge without stealing half the bank. That is a
good repair: the water gets the last word, and the word is quiet.”

**Player:** “Let’s see whether it reaches the far furrows.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and a useful choice of general leveling
  materials; no crafting unlock.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** No early credit; the fitted bundle prevents unrelated
  progress. Nearby accepted party members share repairs and tests. Turn in to Orren
  at the footbridge; S12 unlocks S13 there.

## S13 — The Fair Share

- **Giver:** Orren
- **Location:** Far field, three-way water divider
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Requires S12; final quest in Orren’s chain.
- **Journal:** Balance the repaired flow across three plots and choose where a small
  reserve should go today.

**Offer**

Orren: “The divider serves my beans, Edda’s kitchen patch, and a fallow strip that
holds moisture for late planting. All three need some water. One can take today’s
small reserve. You choose after checking the soil; I can defend any sensible answer
to my neighbors.”

**Player:** “I’ll test each plot before I set the reserve.”

**Objectives**

1. Check the soil at the bean rows, kitchen patch, and fallow strip.
2. Set the three divider stones to establish an even base flow.
3. Direct the reserve to the beans, kitchen patch, or fallow strip.
4. Watch one full pulse pass all three gates.
5. Discuss the choice with Orren.

**Interaction dialogue**

- **Beans — Player:** “The beans are curling first. They get the reserve.”
- Orren: “Then we protect the crop already working.”
- **Kitchen patch — Player:** “The refuge pot serves many people. The patch gets it.”
- Orren: “Then today’s water becomes tomorrow’s supper.”
- **Fallow strip — Player:** “The fallow soil is losing its stored moisture. Send it
  there.”
- Orren: “Then we keep a later planting possible.”

**Progress reminder**

Orren: “Equal base flow first, reserve second. Generosity without measure can empty
the whole channel.”

**Completion**

Orren: “The divider is balanced, and your choice has a reason behind it. Local work
is mostly that: deciding what can wait, then returning before it waits too long.”

**Player:** “Show me the fields next time they change the answer.”

- **Reward intent:** Stronger optional-chain experience and a useful class-leveling
  material selection. All reserve choices give the same reward and lasting outcome.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Soil checks made after S12 count, but the divider
  requires acceptance. Nearby accepted members share flow credit; reserve choices
  are personal. Turn in to Orren. The chain unlocks no main-story feature.

## S14 — A Hive Out of Tune

- **Prerequisite and bundle:** First quest in Nella’s chain. Available after M03 and
  offered at Alderbank before the optional grove outing departs.

**Offer**

Nella: “The east hive sounds wrong. Not angry—thin. The bees keep arriving, circling,
and choosing the older box instead. Watch before you touch. Check the flight path,
the shade screen, and the stand. Bees are excellent witnesses and terrible at giving
statements.”

**Player:** “I’ll follow their evidence and keep my hands to myself.”

**Objectives**

1. Observe three returning bees from the marked safe distance.
2. Inspect the branch over their flight path.
3. Examine the east hive’s shade screen.
4. Check the hive stand for movement.
5. Share the observations with Nella beside the bee row.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Flight path: “Each bee veers around a low twig before reaching the hive.”
- Shade screen: Nella says, “One tie has slipped. It taps the box whenever the wind
  turns.”
- Hive stand: “The rear foot rocks in a shallow wash of soil.”

**Progress reminder**

Nella: “Watch the approach, then the screen, then the feet. A hive tells its troubles
from the outside first.”

**Completion**

Nella: “A crowded path, a tapping screen, and a rocking floor. I would avoid that
house too. We can mend all three without opening the hive.”

**Player:** “Good. The bees can supervise from home.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and useful food or alchemical leveling
  materials.
- **Credit and turn-in notes:** Earlier observation of the marked features counts.
  Nearby accepted party members share inspections. Turn in to Nella at the bee row;
  S14 unlocks S15 there.

## S15 — Better Neighbors for Bees

- **Giver:** Nella
- **Location:** East bee row
- **Prerequisite and bundle:** Requires S14; second quest in Nella’s chain, offered
  on location.
- **Journal:** Make the hive’s surroundings quiet and steady without opening the box
  or harming the bees.

**Offer**

Nella: “We need pruning shears, a fresh screen tie, and a flat stone beneath the rear
foot. Everything is in that basket because I have learned not to carry tools while
bees inspect my sleeves. Work slowly. If they gather near your hands, step back and
let them win.”

**Player:** “A clear path, a quiet screen, and level footing.”

**Objectives**

1. Take Nella’s fitted bee-row basket.
2. Clip the marked twig and place it in the orchard brush pile.
3. Retie the shade screen with the soft cord.
4. Slide the flat stone beneath the hive’s rear foot.
5. Step back and watch returning bees enter normally.

**Interaction dialogue**

- Twig: Nella says, “Clean cut. The tree keeps the branch; the bees keep their road.”
- Screen: “The cloth pulls taut and stops tapping.”
- Footing: Nella says, “Level enough. Hives dislike rocking chairs.”
- Observation: “The next three bees land without circling away.”

**Progress reminder**

Nella: “Basket first. If the hum rises, give them the row until it settles.”

**Completion**

Nella: “Hear that? Busy instead of bothered. People call it silence because they do
not know what a contented hive sounds like.”

**Player:** “I’m beginning to hear the difference.”

- **Reward intent:** Active-class experience and a useful stack of leveling
  consumable ingredients; no beekeeping or crafting profession is assumed.
