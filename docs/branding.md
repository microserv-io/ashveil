# Ashveil brand direction

Ember & Bloom is Ashveil's selected identity direction, approved on 5 September
2026. Its approved wordmark and botanical ember are the exact artwork in the third
panel of `website/assets/source/identity-studies.png`. The palette uses deep forest,
warm ivory, terracotta and harvest gold to hold wonder and danger in the same world.

The public `ashveil-wordmark.svg` and `ashveil-emblem.svg` files are SVG viewport
containers around embedded, byte-identical source PNG data. Their viewports expose
only the selected panel's wordmark or emblem while retaining the painted parchment
ground. They are not vector paths. A redrawn production vector, recoloured variants
and a single-colour small-size mark are deferred brand work.

## Type

- **Alegreya** is the supporting display face for page titles and editorial moments.
  It is not claimed as the source of the approved wordmark. It is self-hosted from
  `website/public/fonts/alegreya-variable.ttf` under the SIL Open Font License
  included beside it.
- **Source Sans 3** is the navigation and reading face. It is self-hosted from
  `website/public/fonts/source-sans-3-variable.ttf` under the SIL Open Font License
  included beside it.

## Artwork and provenance

The two public images are early visual-development concept art generated with
OpenAI's built-in `image_gen` tool on 5 September 2026. They are concept direction,
not screenshots or a promise of final game content.

- `ember-and-bloom-world.png` explores a luminous shared fantasy landscape, viewed
  by a small travelling party, with painterly foliage, cream stone and ember-gold
  details.
- `where-the-colour-fades.png` explores Ashveil's central contrast: a warm living
  settlement and flowering landscape giving way to a still, ash-drained world
  across a stone bridge.

Exact generation prompts and source provenance are recorded with the source images
in `website/assets/source/README.md`.

## Usage

Use the approved wordmark and emblem on their original parchment ground. Do not
retype the wordmark in Alegreya, recolour the emblem or describe these containers as
vector artwork. Keep generous clear space until production vector rules and a tested
minimum size exist. Never present concept artwork as captured gameplay.
