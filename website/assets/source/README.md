# Ember & Bloom concept artwork

Rocco selected identity study 03, **Ember & Bloom**, on 5 September 2026. The
botanical ember, substantial serif lettering, warm earthy palette and welcoming
shared-world illustration are the chosen direction. These are website concept
illustrations, not screenshots or commitments about locations, races or classes.

The two final source PNGs were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 5 September
2026. No CLI fallback was used. Runtime image derivatives are generated by the
website build. Keep these sources unchanged so later creative revisions can be
compared with their originals. No third-party game artwork was used as a reference.

## `ember-and-bloom-world.png`

Reference: the rightmost panel of `identity-studies.png`, the exploration board
generated in this task and retained here as source provenance, not public artwork;
the user explicitly selected that panel. The board established a leaf-and-ember
emblem, bold warm serif wordmark, green/ivory/terracotta/gold palette and a party
overlooking a river valley.

Exact generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: panoramic website hero artwork for ASHVEIL, an original painterly mythic fantasy MMORPG. Reference image role: selected art direction is ONLY the RIGHTMOST third column '03 / Ember & Bloom', specifically its bottom landscape. Create a finished standalone wide landscape illustration expanding that bottom-right scene. No board, lettering, logos, borders or text. Four or five travelling companions, adult naturally proportioned varied silhouettes, seen from behind in lower right foreground on a grassy ridge, sharing a view of a vast colourful inhabited valley. One has cream tunic with teal shoulder cloth and ochre cape, others earthy woven travel clothing, wood staff or pack; no weapon closeups. River meandering through village of cream-stone and timber homes with terracotta roofs, small bridges, distant fantastical hill settlement, golden trees, big soft blue sky. Far distant at the extreme horizon a subtle pale ash-grey region where colour is draining, a quiet threat rather than smoke and destruction. Broad hand-painted colour shapes, soft two/three-tone form shading, tactile nature, expressive readable silhouettes. Warm fantasy illustration, painterly anime-adjacent clarity without hard outlines, no photoreal rendering, no chibi, no franchise-specific recognizable characters or architecture. Preserve the rightmost reference's pastoral warmth, lush forest greens, warm ivory, amber sun, terracotta accents and welcoming sense of a shared world. Compose in wide 16:9 or wider cinematic aspect ratio, with companions limited to lower right quarter, beautiful natural landscape and darker forest/tree framing at far left, open valley center for visual breathing space. Premium evocative painterly key art with visible brushwork, highly coherent terrain and river, not a generic game screenshot.

## `where-the-colour-fades.png`

Reference: `ember-and-bloom-world.png`, used to keep the same world and surface
language across the preset artwork.

Exact generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: second reusable ASHVEIL website illustration, 'Where the colour fades'. Reference image is the approved warm painterly world key art; preserve its broad painterly colour shapes, cream-stone and timber architecture, terracotta roofs, lush forest greens, golden sunlight and sense of natural wonder. Generate a different scene within the same original mythic fantasy MMORPG world: intimate panoramic river crossing with an old low cream-stone bridge, a modest wayside shelter with ochre woven fabric, vivid wildflowers and green leaves in the foreground. Across the river the grove gradually becomes powdery ash-grey, leaves suspended still, shapes fading into a pale veil. The transition is subtle and haunting, with recognisable shapes retaining their form but losing colour and motion; not a burning forest or dark horror. One small cloaked adult traveller at the bridge for scale, back view, no detailed face. The left two-thirds of composition glow with life, right third softly fades to ash. Warm tactile fantasy illustration with visible brushwork and strong readable forms, soft anime-adjacent simplified form shading, no photorealism, no chibi, no thick outlines, no franchise motifs. Wide landscape 16:9. No text, logo, border or labels.
